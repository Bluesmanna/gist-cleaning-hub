1. Welcome to GIST Cleaning
gist (jĭst) (n): the main or essential part of a matter.


The GIST in GIST Cleaning stands for "Getting Into Stuff Together". It may sound sentimental, but it encapsulates our approach, in both acronym and definition: we approach clients’ needs collaboratively to distill the essence of what we can provide for their lives, their homes, or their businesses.
GIST Cleaning is a small, residential-only cleaning, organization and consultancy business built on trust, reliability, and care. Our clients invite us into their homes and places of business, and that trust is something we take very seriously.
As a team member, you are not just cleaning - you are helping create calm, comfort, and safety for real people. We aim to treat our clients and our employees with respect, transparency, and humanity.

2. Job Duties for Your Position
Residential Cleaning Technician
From the usual spit-n'-shine to that bathroom no one talks about, and everything in between, we provide our customers with service they can rely on and a clean home they can be satisfied with. We perform regular upkeep cleaning, tidying, deep cleaning projects, and move-in/move-out cleaning. 


Job duties for each of our available customer packages include, but are not exclusively limited to, the following: 


Basic Cleaning Package
* Floor care: sweeping, vacuuming, mopping, everyday messes and staining.
* Hard surfaces: dusting, sanitizing wipe-down, wood surface care. 
* Furniture: surface cleaning, vacuuming, everyday messes, light upholstery care.
* Bathrooms: everyday upkeep, including toilets and baths/showers.
* Basic miscellany: windows, taking out trash, sweeping off steps/porches, cobweb removal, etc. 
* Deep scrubbing: lime/hard water/gunk, kitchen grease buildup, children's wall graffiti, etc.
* Upon request:
   * Low-use/low-visibility surfaces: walls, baseboards, decorative shelves, cabinet shelves, etc.
   * Pet messes: changing litter boxes, cleaning and deodorizing everyday or chronic "potty areas".
   * High-detail miscellany: dusting window blinds, trophy shelves, sanitizing movie cases, etc.
Add-Ons
These add-on duties are listed on our website, and can be added to basic cleaning services at client request. You will be informed if these duties are requested by any client. 
* Tidying: Includes pick-up of dishes, toys, clothes, everyday pet chaos, movies, books, papers, and other clutter. (Also includes labelled put-away bins upon client request; these will be charged for and provided for you by GIST Cleaning management.)
* Washing: Includes washing dishes and/or laundry to client specifications and preferences.
* Pet Poop Cleanup. Charged per occurrence, and adequate pickup supplies must be provided by client. If you have an issue that makes you unable/unwilling to provide this add-on, please consult with management. 




"White Glove" and Move-In/Move-Out Cleaning
* Any necessary tasks from Basic Cleaning Package above, plus:
* Cleaning inside and under major appliances; ovens, refrigerators, washers & dryers, etc.
* Scrubbing walls, cabinets, shelves and baseboards. 
* Dusting & vacuuming uncommon areas (heaters and air vents, ceiling fan blades, etc.)
* Minor mold or mildew elimination.

3. Supplies and Transportation
While it is often industry standard for residential cleaning companies to make their employees purchase their own supplies, and the cost of transportation is often uncompensated, we like to do things differently. Despite being a small business, we make sure we can afford to keep our employees making a living and focusing on quality work. 
Supplies
Your initial cleaning kit will be provided for you by GIST Cleaning. This includes your vacuum, mopping hardware, cleaning chemicals, rags and brushes, razors and scrapers, carrying totes, and any other necessary supplies. We want to set you up for success from the start!


You will also be supplied with a company debit card. $75 per pay period will be allotted to this account for supply costs, and you may spend the available balance on replenishing your supplies however you see fit. You must keep your receipts for your supply purchases; these will be given to your GIST Cleaning manager, due at the end of each pay period.
Transportation
Each pay period, GIST Cleaning will provide you a Visa gift card in the amount of $50. This gift card is to be used to offset gas costs. You will be given a new gift card for the new pay period at the time you turn in your supply receipts.


If your employment with GIST Cleaning is terminated for any reason, all cards given to you, along with any supplies purchased with business funds, must be returned directly to your GIST Cleaning manager.

4. Scheduling & Timeliness
Hours
Most employees in the Residential Cleaning Technician role begin working part-time (approximately 20–25 hours per week). Hours may vary depending on client demand, scheduling needs, and seasonal changes.
Additional hours, including the opportunity to move toward full-time work, may be offered by mutual agreement but are not guaranteed.
Scheduling Expectations
* Work schedules are set in advance whenever possible
* Reliability and punctuality are very important in residential cleaning, as our clients rely on us to maintain the cleanliness of their homes and often arrange their schedules around our services
* If you are unable to work a scheduled shift, notify your client and your GIST Cleaning manager as soon as possible
   * There is no penalty for reasonably cancelling a shift, but doing so chronically or without good reason may be grounds for disciplinary action or termination
   * If you need to cancel a shift, please make every effort to reschedule the client’s cleaning according to their availability
Timeliness
Each client has an assigned recurring time slot, scheduled according to their preferences. Given the variable nature of cleaning, there is some flexibility allowed regarding your arrival time. 
* Our timeliness policy dictates that you may arrive up to 15-30 minutes outside of your client’s scheduled start time. 
* If you are running any later than this, you must reach out directly to your client to let them know and ensure that your late arrival will not interfere with their schedule
   * If your late arrival will interfere with a client’s schedule, apologize and discuss rescheduling or rearranging cleaning priorities with the client. 
   * There is no penalty for reasonably running late, but doing so chronically or without good reason may be grounds for disciplinary action or termination

5. Pay & Timekeeping
Pay
Employees are paid hourly and must accurately record all hours worked using the company’s timekeeping system. Our current starting rate for the Residential Cleaning Technician position is $18/hour. 
Overtime is paid according to federal and Idaho law for hours worked over 40 in a work week.
Reviews & Raises
Employees receive a formal performance review at approximately six (6) months, with additional reviews every six months thereafter.
Reviews consider:
* Quality of work
* Reliability and attendance
* Client feedback
* Communication and teamwork
Raises are discretionary and based on performance and business needs. For detailed information on performance reviews and raises, please see your Employment Agreement, section 4.
Paid Time Off
Paid time off is earned at a rate of 1 hour per 25 hours worked. You may use your PTO for any of the following:
* Hours missing due to sickness or absence
* Vacation time
   * Vacation time that is more than 2 consecutive days must be approved by your GIST Manager. Please reach out at least 3 weeks in advance, or your request may not be approved. 
   * You will be responsible for coordinating your absence with your clients. Please ensure your shifts are covered whenever possible; reach out to your GIST Cleaning manager if you require assistance.  


If you choose to terminate employment with GIST Cleaning, you may claim any unused PTO balance on your final paycheck, provided you give a minimum of two weeks’ notice and work your scheduled shifts until the time of your termination. For detailed information on this policy, please refer to your Employment Agreement, section 12.  
Timekeeping
* All time worked must be recorded
* Working off the clock is not permitted
* Falsifying time records may result in discipline or termination
We use a service called Gusto for our payroll and time tracking. You should have received an invitation for onboarding with Gusto; if you have not received this invitation, please reach out to your GIST Cleaning manager. You can log your hours through Gusto on your mobile device, as well as edit logged hours if needed. 


Only hours worked on location may be logged; transportation time to and from clients’ homes do not count towards daily hours worked. Gusto uses geofencing capabilities to assure accuracy, ease and liability management for both employees and management; you will be required to turn on Location settings for Gusto in your mobile device for this capability to function.


We ask that you log your hours promptly and accurately, with as few timecard edits as possible. If you do need to edit your timecard, you will be asked to enter a note explaining the reasons for the edit; please be thorough and succinct. Excessive time card edits may be grounds for corrective action.

6. Conduct & Appearance
Residential cleaning takes place in the homes and personal spaces of our clients, sometimes while they are home. As such, it is of paramount importance that our employees’ conduct be respectful, accountable, and appropriate. 
Conduct 
We expect all team members to behave professionally and respectfully. This includes:
* Upholding client confidentiality standards (see your Employment Agreement document, section 8.)
* No theft, dishonesty, or misuse of property
* Respectful communication with clients and coworkers
* Following company policies and instructions
The following behaviors are considered unacceptable, and are grounds for immediate termination:
* Harassment of any kind
* Violent or aggressive behavior
* Sexually and/or romantically inappropriate or forward behavior towards a client or any person in the client’s home
* Smoking or vaping at work, either interior or exterior. 
   * If you require nicotine, smoke only in your own vehicle or other public spaces while off-site.
* Being intoxicated while at work. (See your Employment Agreement document, section 11.)
   * We also acknowledge that, due to our company’s proximity to Washington state borders, that recreational cannabis usage may be a part of some employees’ lives. A positive result for cannabis on a drug test in and of itself is not grounds for immediate termination, but intoxication via substance use of any sort while on shift is still considered unacceptable and is grounds for termination.  
The following behaviors are not grounds for immediate termination, but may be subject to corrective action: 
* Excessive use of cellular or mobile devices, including phone conversation, such that it is interfering with quality or timeliness of work or is noted by clients
* Excessive talkativeness with clients, such that it is interfering with quality or timeliness of work or intruding upon client’s personal time
* Loud or disruptive behavior (e.g. loud or emotionally intense phone conversations, loud singing or dancing while clients are present)
* Any other behavior that consistently interferes with quality or timeliness of work, or is noted as concerning or disruptive by clients


Please be aware: in the state of Idaho, it is legal for one party to record another without notice. This means that, at any time, a client may have cameras in their home and is under no legal obligation to inform either you or GIST Cleaning management. This means that appropriate conduct is paramount, whether or not a client is present.
Appearance
Though cleaning is dirty work, it is also important that we present ourselves with appropriate cleanliness and professionality. This includes:
* Clean, practical clothing suitable for physical work
* Good personal hygiene
* Clothing should be respectful and appropriate for entering private home

7. Employee Safety, Wellness & Physical Expectations
Physical Expectations
Residential cleaning is physically demanding work. Tasks may include bending, kneeling, lifting, standing for extended periods, scrubbing, and using cleaning products. Employees are expected to be able to perform these tasks with minimal difficulty and no anticipated damage to their body.
Safety Expectations
* Follow all safety and training instructions on all labels, documents and instructional manuals
* Use gloves or protective equipment when required or advisable
* Never mix cleaning chemicals
* Report injuries, accidents, or unsafe conditions immediately
Please inform us if you have any allergies or sensitivities to certain cleaning chemicals, and we will do our best to accommodate you. 
Your safety matters to us. Please speak up if something feels unsafe.
Wellness
Employee wellness is a top priority for GIST Cleaning, and maintaining a good work-life balance is highly encouraged. If you find yourself ill or unwell and needing to rest and recover, please consider doing so and rescheduling with your client(s) if possible.


Client wellness is also a top priority. We do serve elderly clients, immunocompromised clients, and/or clients with small children. If you are running a fever and/or are confirmed with a contagious illness (e.g. COVID, influenza, norovirus), it is your responsibility to inform both your GIST Cleaning manager and your client(s)  each day you continue to run a fever and/or remain contagious. 


We always prefer to keep contagious illnesses at home, but understand that some employees may be reluctant to lose out on hours. If you wish to work while contagiously ill, you must receive consent from your client(s) for the day before performing your cleaning duties at their home and mask for the duration of your shift(s).
