# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES -

## **General** (Residential Cleaning Technician)

***These are standard operating procedures and guidelines for GIST Cleaning employees in the Residential Cleaning Technician position to follow throughout all homes.** If you have any questions, or have any accommodation requests that cause conflict with one of the procedures outlined herein, please reach out to your GIST Cleaning manager.*

### **Client Homes**

#### Security Procedures

Each home will have its own security procedures; please refer to the individual SOP for the household you are cleaning, and reach out to your client directly if you have any questions.

#### Use of Client Resources

Reasonable use of the following client resources is permitted:

* Restroom facilities as needed  
* Seats or tables, as needed for breaks (see *Breaks*, below.)  
* Microwave, if needed for lunches and if wiped out afterward (please avoid microwaving seafood or other particularly pungent foods)  
* Water for drinking, from faucets or fridge dispenser if applicable  
* Electrical outlets for charging small personal devices  
* Paper towels or toilet paper for cleaning purposes  
* Wi-Fi (see *Wi-Fi,* below)

#### Wi-Fi

Some homes have WiFi available for use. For security purposes, this information is not included in any SOP’s; if you would like to utilize a client’s Wi-Fi, please reach out to them directly to request access.

### **Breaks**

GIST Cleaning has no official break policy, given that work hours are not continuous/consecutive. Please take meal breaks as-needed; you may do so between clients, or order a lunch to your location/bring a lunch with you. Please limit lunch breaks to 30 min at maximum. You may also take short sitting breaks as-needed to accommodate bodily strain. Smoking breaks are allowed only off-shift and off-site (see your *Employee Handbook,* section 6.) Excessive breaks, such that it is interfering with the ability to perform tasks thoroughly or in a timely fashion, may be cause for corrective action.

### **Supplies**

**RECOMMENDED SUPPLIES LIST**  
Your GIST Cleaning manager should provide you with your initial cleaning kit. As you work through the supplies provided and begin to replace what you have used/purchase further supplies with the company card provided, we recommend keeping the following supplies on hand. Some are required, and can/will be provided for your starting cleaning kit, and some are not required but are recommended to have if needed.   
**REQUIRED:**

* Vacuum  
* Damp mop  
* Rags  
* All-purpose and/or sanitizing surface cleaner  
* Toilet bowl cleaner  
* Sanitizing wipes (for toilets & surrounding areas *only*)  
* Glass cleaner  
* Bona or Murphy’s floor detergent (for wood floors)  
* Swiffer dusters, or non-brand equivalent  
* PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) as appropriate; gloves, face mask, protective glasses or goggles, etc.

**RECOMMENDED**:

* Grit cloth or Pumie pumice stones (for toilet rings & mineral staining)  
* Comet, or other mild abrasive cleaner  
* Swiffer mop & pads (good for tile floors or areas with high or visible hair content)  
* Furniture polish (we recommend Old English or Orange-Glo for wood furniture)  
* Stainless steel polish   
* Bristled scrubbing brushes (for cracks & crevices, textured surfaces, grout)  
* Razor and/or scraper  
* Paper towels

If any of these supplies were not provided in your starting kit and you would like to use them, you may purchase them at your liberty with your company supplies account - or, if additional desired supplies represent a cost that will infringe on your ability to replace necessities, reach out to your GIST Cleaning manager to discuss reimbursement or having the company purchase it for you.

***Follow any and all instructions listed on any and all cleaning supplies.** This includes usage instructions and guidelines, dilution instructions, maintenance protocols, and safety requirements, **including any recommended PPE.** Failure to do so will result in corrective action or, in extreme cases, immediate termination.*

 ***If you have any doubts or unanswered questions** regarding supply use, please use an appropriate internet search engine to find answers - or, if needed, reach out to your GIST Cleaning manager.*   
   
**Spray Bottles**

* Re-use spray bottles. Wherever possible or applicable, we heavily prefer purchasing concentrated solutions and refilling bottles as we go. This reduces waste, and is more cost effective when purchasing cleaners. **Follow all dilution instructions and mixing precautions.**

**Brushes, Sponges and Pads**

* Utilize only **non-scratch** abrasive pads & sponges. These are typically colored blue across most major brands, but always check to be sure.  
* **Rinse brushes** after use, to prevent caking and/or carrying residue or bacteria between locations.  
* Replace any brushes, sponges or pads as needed once they begin to lose abrasion or show signs of wear and tear that affects their usefulness.

**Razors & Scrapers**

* **Always follow safety precautions when using bladed implements.**  
* **Carefully** wipe the blade clean/dry before retracting and replacing in your kit to prevent rusting or dulling  
* Replace any blades that have become dull, rusty, or bent. 

**All Purpose Cleaner**

* Ensure you are following all dilution and mixing instructions where applicable.   
* Best used for surfaces not requiring full sanitization. (Examples: kitchen counters, microwaves, living space surfaces, sinks

**Sanitizing/Disinfectant Cleaner**

* Ensure you are following all dilution and mixing instructions where applicable.  
* **If using bleach**-containing disinfectant cleaners, take extra care not to mix with any other chemicals as bleach can be volatile. **Take care/do not use around non-colorsafe fabrics.**  
* Best used for hard surfaces in high-microbe traffic areas, such as bathrooms and kitchen sinks.   
* Can be used on almost any hard surface if preferred, but can be more caustic than some all-purpose cleaners; follow product precautions and use best judgment. 

**Toilet Bowl Cleaner**

* **If using bleach**-containing toilet bowl cleaners, take extra care not to mix with any other chemicals as bleach can be volatile.   
* After scrubbing, flush multiple times until all residue is gone, including from under bowl. Residue left unrinsed and/or unflushed can erode ceramic, or make pets sick. 

**Sanitizing Wipes**

* **We highly prefer to use sanitizing wipes for toilets** rather than rags and spray cleaners; no lint or fabric residue is left behind, and it reduces chances of carrying harmful bacteria to other areas.  
* To reduce waste, **use only for toilets** and surrounding floor areas, or anywhere else that may have come into contact with human or animal waste. 

**Murphy’s Detergent**

* Murphy’s Detergent is a concentrate. Ensure you are following all dilution and mixing instructions.   
* Mix an appropriate amount of Murphy’s and warm water into a spray bottle according to dilution instructions, and spray on well-finished wood floors before damp-mopping with an appropriate damp-fabric mop. 

**Furniture Polish**

* Use according to product instructions.   
* If using Old English or Orange-Glo (which we recommend for any household with older wooden furniture), use only on items that are plain finished wood. Do not use on items that are painted wood, or coated/composite wood.

**Stainless Steel Polish**

* Use according to product instructions.  
* We recommend using a microfiber towel for stainless steel polishing. 

**Comet**

* Comet is the most price-effective and commonly used mild abrasive cleaner, and also contains bleach, so it serves multiple purposes and is our recommended powder cleanser.   
* Use for sinks, tubs, glass stovetops, and treated stainless steel faucets to cut through grease, grime, and everyday hard water buildup. You can also use to remove stains from some countertops.  
* Use according to product instructions.   
* **Do not overscrub** or apply excessive pressure, as it may result in scratching on some surfaces.   
* For a more abrasive cleaner for tougher jobs, we recommend **The Pink Stuff** paste.

**Swiffer Mop**

* Use according to product instructions.   
* Best used for tile or linoleum floors, or areas with excessive hair/other potential left-behind matter  
* Replace pads with appropriate frequency, and watch for streaking

### Surfaces

**General**

* Utilize **dry rags** with an appropriate cleaner or polish **on living space surfaces** as needed to remove fingerprints or smudging or polish/condition surfaces that require it. (Examples: entertainment centers, end tables, decorative surfaces, shelves) This helps to reduce the chances of moisture streaking/staining on surfaces that do not require water.  
* Utilize **wet rags** with an appropriate cleaner **on kitchen, bathroom and dining room** surfaces. **Water is essential** to catching sticky spots, spills, grease, and food residue with the most efficiency - reducing the “elbow grease” required and lessening the chances of missing individual spots - and gives you least chance of leaving undesirable bits of streaking or residue behind. **Wet your rag thoroughly, wring damp,** and then **rinse as needed** as you go to remove crumbs, grease or residue.   
* Utilize **non-scratch abrasive pads** (typically blue) for the initial scrub of sinks & faucets, following up with a rag for the rinse & dry; and as needed for everyday mineral buildup, stuck food, etc. **Exercise best judgment**; if you’re not sure whether a surface will be damaged by an abrasive pad, forego utilizing one until you’ve done some research. A quick Google search will usually tell you what you need to know\!

**Stainless Steel**

* **For steel sinks and faucets:** use an appropriate cleaner and rag/pad for the level of grease, grime, or hard water buildup in/on the sink. Wet wipe or rinse after initial scrub, then wipe dry. Polish if needed/desired once surface is dry (stainless steel polish combined with extra moisture tends to leave residue.) **If using abrasive cleaner**, do not over-scrub or apply excessive pressure.   
* **For appliances:** use an appropriate cleaner and rag/pad to clean off any grease, grime or residue. Polish once surface is dry.

**Wood Floors**

* **Do not wet/sponge mop\!** It will damage and warp wood flooring. Damp mopping (Swiffer, Bona or other fabric pad mop) is okay, so long as appropriate detergent is used and moisture level does not leave trails or puddles.   
* **Only** use floor cleaners that are explicitly labelled as being safe for wood floors.  
* **If you notice warping or damaged finish** when you first begin cleaning a client’s house, please take pictures and ensure the client is aware so any potential liability for damage can be mitigated.

**Wood Furniture**

* **Only** use moistened rags if finish is fully intact. Otherwise, utilize dry rags.  
* **Only** use wood-safe cleaners.   
* Utilize oil-based wood polish regularly to keep wood lustrous and maintain older wood.

**Painted Walls/Cabinets/Baseboards**

* **Do not use** abrasive pads, as it will wear away most paint.  

### Appliances

**Vacuum**

* Follow manufacturer instructions for regular maintenance, including  
  * Emptying dust cup when full  
  * Blowing/beating out and regularly washing dust and air filters  
  * Periodically removing wound-up hair, string or other materials from brush roll  
* Utilize appropriate vacuum settings for the floor you are cleaning  
* Utilize attachments as necessary for baseboards, corners, upholstery, vents, etc.  
* **If using a client vacuum**, always exercise caution and care.  
* Report any issues or breakages to your GIST Cleaning manager immediately. 

**Mop**

* Thoroughly rinse/wring mop after use to avoid mildewy odors  
* Thoroughly hot-rinse fabric pads for damp mops in between households, and wash regularly with laundry.   
* Report any issues or breakages to your GIST Cleaning manager. 

**Steam Cleaner or Other Misc Hardware**

* Follow manufacturer instructions for use and maintenance.  
* Take care using a steam cleaner on painted surfaces, and avoid using on wood.  
* Report any issues or breakages to your GIST Cleaning manager.
