# Skill: GIST Cleaning — the business

GIST Cleaning ("Getting Into Stuff Together"; gist = "the main or essential part of a matter") is a small, owner-run cleaning and organization service in Moscow, Idaho, serving Moscow and Pullman on the Palouse. Owner/manager: Dustin Sipes. Contact: gistcleaning@gmail.com, 208-310-0809. Licensed and insured LLC.

## Values
- Quality work, individual needs, comprehensive approach (Root Cause Analysis: assess the mess, find causes, plan, then organize so it stays fixed).
- Queer-friendly, disability-friendly, mental-health-inclusive, and a **no-shame** space. Never judge a client's home. "Rescue cleaning" exists for homes that got away from people.
- Tone: warm, plain, a little playful ("spit-n'-shine", "doom drawers", "I'm not your mother"-free tidying), never corporate.

## Services and prices (2026)
- Basic Cleaning Package: $37/hour (raised from $30 on Jan 12, 2026). Floors, hard surfaces, furniture, bathrooms, windows, trash, cobwebs, deep scrubbing. On request: walls/baseboards/shelves, pet messes/litter, high-detail items.
- "White Glove" & Move-Out: from $50/hour (inside appliances, walls/cabinets/baseboards, vents, minor mold/mildew).
- "Rescue Cleaning": from $40/hour (assess the mess → plan → reset → organize).
- Residential Organization: from $45/hour, basic cleaning of the project space included.
- Commercial cleaning & "GIST for Business" organization (files, obsolete equipment, duplicate inventory, digital clutter, outdated SOPs): small-business rates, quoted case by case.
- Add-ons: Tidying +$2/hr; Washing (dishes/laundry) +$3/hr, or +$2/hr with Tidying; Pet care $15 per check-in ($20 non-clients); Pet poop cleanup $30 per occurrence.
- Frequencies: weekly, biweekly, monthly, as-needed, one-time, multi-day projects.
- Payment: Venmo, Zelle or Square, due within 24 hours of the invoice.

## Cancellation policy
48+ hours notice: no fee. 24–48 hours: $20. Under 24 hours / same day: half of the scheduled cleaning time. Exceptions for emergencies and sickness — just communicate. Rescheduling may take a week or more because the schedule is usually full.
