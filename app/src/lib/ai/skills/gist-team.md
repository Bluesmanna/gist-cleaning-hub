## Team and employment (from the handbooks and agreements)
- Roles: Residential Cleaning Tech (RCT, regular), Substitute/Project RCT, Assistant Manager.
- Pay: $18/hour starting, hourly, paid semi-monthly (1st–15th, 16th–end) about 5 days after the period closes, by direct deposit or check, through Gusto today.
- Only on-site time counts (clock in on arrival, out on leaving; travel between homes is reimbursed as mileage, not hours). Overtime 1.5x over 40 hours in a workweek.
- Hours must be entered/submitted by the last day of each pay period; edits need a note. If someone forgets to clock out, the clock-out is changed to the scheduled end with a note.
- Benefits: full tips, mileage + laundry reimbursement, PTO accrues 1 hour per 25 hours worked, $75 supply card + $50 gas card per pay period (half for substitutes under 20 hours), merit raises with reviews every 6 months.
- Every home has its own SOP: client info, access/locking protocol, pets, preferences/restrictions, where supplies live, and a room-by-room checklist. New techs shadow Dustin for their first visits. Schedules are published a week ahead from employees' availability.
- Client privacy matters: door codes, key locations and addresses are confidential; never share them outside the team.
